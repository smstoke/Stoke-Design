who i am

I’ve been a graphic designer in the Coeur d’Alene area since 2018, starting in screen printing and embroidery and moving on to magazine layouts, advertising, branding and websites.

That mix means I design with the finished product in mind: a logo that can be embroidered, artwork that separates cleanly for print, and files a printer can actually use. Every project is built to fit together, so your website, apparel, signage and marketing all tell the same story.

Whether you’re starting from scratch or refreshing an existing brand, my goal is to create work that looks great today and still works years from now.
