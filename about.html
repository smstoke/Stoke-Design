Graphic Designer in Coeur d’Alene, Idaho About Stoke Design

who i am

I’m a graphic designer who came up through the print shop. I started in 2018 at a screen printing and embroidery company in Coeur d’Alene, where I learned what makes artwork hold up once it’s on a shirt, a hat or a sign: simple shapes, clean color separations and files a printer can use without guessing.

From there I moved into magazine layout, advertising and branding. Over four years I designed and prepared regional magazines for print, and built logos, brand guidelines and websites for clients across North Idaho and the Spokane area.

Stoke Design is where all of that comes together. A logo has to look good, but it also has to be simple enough to embroider, scale to any size and come with the files a printer needs. I build brand systems and guidelines so your business looks the same on a website, a sign, a shirt and a magazine page.

I believe good design should make your business look established, build trust and be easy to recognize. Whether you’re starting from scratch or refreshing an existing brand, my goal is to create work that looks great today and still works years from now.

experience

2022 – 2026

Magazine, Ads & Brand Design

I designed magazine page layouts and print advertisements for several regional publications in InDesign, Photoshop and Illustrator. I placed all of the editorial and ad content in each issue and prepared the final files for the printer, and met every print deadline.

Alongside the magazines I created logos, brand guidelines and other marketing assets for clients, and designed websites with layouts and graphics built for both desktop and mobile. I also set up AI workflows to handle the repetitive parts of blogging, newsletter production and web updates, which left more time for hands-on design work.

2018 – 2022

Screen Printing & Embroidery

I created screen print graphics and vectorized low quality customer artwork for production. I handled prepress, including color separations, printing film and preparing files for the vinyl cutter, and worked directly with customers on proofs and revisions through final approval.

I also updated the company website and helped with sales, customer service and shop production, so I know how a design behaves on the press floor, not just on screen.

2019 – 2020

E-commerce & Marketing Design

I designed web and social media ads that stayed consistent with existing branding, built email campaigns in Mailchimp, photographed and edited products and uploaded listings to Shopify. I also edited videos and created animated intros and thumbnails.

For the same owner I worked as a production artist, setting up graphics for screen printing and printing color separations onto film.
